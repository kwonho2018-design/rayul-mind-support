# Support

My Music Cassette

Effective: October 14, 2026

A retro offline player for music files you import on your iPhone.

### How do I import music?

Tap Import in Library and select MP3, M4A (AAC or ALAC), or WAV files in Files. iCloud Drive files may require an initial download. DRM-protected files are not supported.

### Where are the three included songs?

Choose 日本語 in Settings → App language. The three original Mizuki Aoi songs, Japanese lyrics and artwork are available only when Japanese is selected. Personal imported music works in every language. Deleted bundled music can be restored from Japanese Settings.

### How do I use playback, lyrics and player styles?

Select a song and tap the mini player. Use the playback keys or tap the model name to choose from five designs. Scroll down for lyrics, the sleep timer and the queue.

### Why is there no sound?

Check playback state, media volume, and whether Bluetooth or AirPlay is connected to another device. Disconnecting headphones may pause playback.

### How is my music stored or deleted?

The app stores internal copies of imported files. Delete individual tracks in Library or clear all data in Settings. Original files outside the app are kept. Removing the app may delete its copies, so keep your originals separately.
