# Privacy Policy

My Music Cassette

Effective: October 14, 2026

## Collection and transmission

The app uses no accounts, advertising, tracking tools, third-party analytics SDKs or developer-operated backend. It does not automatically send music files, titles, lyrics, artwork, playback history or preferences to the developer or third parties.

## Information stored on your device

Imported audio copies, metadata, artwork, cassettes, favorites, playback position and language/display preferences are stored locally for playback and library management. The three included songs, lyrics and images are bundled with the app and shown when the in-app language is Japanese.

## File providers and backups

Choosing a file from iCloud Drive or another provider may cause that provider to download it. The app has no cloud sync. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your system settings. Apple or the file provider’s policies apply to those services.

## Retention and deletion

Delete individual tracks or clear library data in Settings to remove internal copies and playback history. Language/display preferences and original files outside the app remain. Changing the app language does not delete music files. Removing the app may remove its copies.

## Contact and website

If you email support, the address and message you provide are processed to answer your request. There is no need to send music files or sensitive information. Opening a support webpage may allow its hosting provider to process ordinary connection data for delivery and security. The app does not send its music or playback data to the site.
